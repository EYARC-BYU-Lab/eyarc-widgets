Interest rate names
click to reveal
TermDefinition
Discount rateThe interest rate used to convert a future amount of money into its value today.
Required rate of returnThe minimum return an investor expects to earn before choosing to invest.
Nominal rateThe stated interest rate before considering inflation.
Real rateThe rate of return after adjusting for inflation. Approximately equal to the nominal rate minus inflation.
Annual percentage rate (APR)The stated annual interest rate before accounting for the effects of compounding.
Compounding vs. discounting
click to reveal
ConceptDefinition
Simple interestInterest earned only on the original amount (the principal) invested or borrowed.
Compound interestInterest earned on both the original amount (the principal) and previously earned interest.
CompoundingMoving money forward in time to determine its future value (PV → FV).
DiscountingMoving money backward in time to determine its present value (FV → PV).
Sign conventionPV and FV must always have opposite signs when using a financial calculator or Excel because one represents cash out and the other represents cash in.
Cash flow types
click to reveal
TypeDefinition
Lump sumSingle payment made or received at one point in time.
AnnuityA series of equal payments made or received at regular intervals.
PerpetuityA series of equal payments that continues indefinitely.
PV = PMT ÷ r
r = interest rate or required rate of return
Growing perpetuityPayments continue indefinitely and grow at a constant rate.
PV = PMT ÷ (r − g)
r = interest rate  ·  g = growth rate
Uneven cash flowsA series of cash flows where the amounts vary from one period to the next.

Your friends are planning a trip to Europe after graduation. You know you'll need to save to have enough money for the trip. Fortunately, you already have $2,000 saved that you can safely dedicate toward this goal. You also think you can save $100 per month. If you start saving now, how long will it take you to reach your savings goal?

Consider the following scenario:

  • You have a lump sum of $2,000 already saved today
  • An additional $100 is contributed each month
  • The account earns 6.0% APR
  • Your goal is to accumulate $5,000
  • How long will it take to reach your goal?

Enter the following values into your financial calculator in any order, then solve for N.

Reminder: Clear your calculator before starting a new TVM problem to avoid using values left over from a previous calculation.

Press N to solve.

N ≈ 25.6 months

Converting the months into years: 25.6 months ÷ 12 months ≈ 2.1 years.

Important: Because contributions are made monthly, make sure your calculator is set to P/YR = 12 before entering the TVM variables. The calculator will return N in months.
PV and PMT are entered as negative because money is flowing out of your pocket. FV is positive because it is money flowing back to you. The calculator solves for how many periods are needed for the account balance to grow from its current value to the $5,000 savings goal.
It will take you approximately 25.6 months, or about 2.1 years, to save enough money for the trip.

When the number of periods (N) is unknown, we must solve for the exponent in the TVM formula. Logarithms allow us to isolate and solve for that exponent.

The combined FV equation
FV = PV × (1 + r)ⁿ + PMT × [((1 + r)ⁿ − 1) / r]
Step 1 — Put the rate in the same timeframe as the recurring payments
6% ÷ 12 = 0.5%
Step 2 — Substitute the known values
5,000 = 2,000 × (1.005)ⁿ + 100 × [((1.005)ⁿ − 1) / 0.005]
Step 3 — Simplify by letting x = (1.005)ⁿ
5,000 = 2,000x + 100 [(x − 1) / 0.005]
5,000 = 2,000x + 20,000(x − 1)
5,000 = 2,000x + 20,000x − 20,000
25,000 = 22,000x

x = 25,000 / 22,000
x = 1.13636
Step 4 — Solve for n using logarithms
(1.005)ⁿ = 1.13636
n = ln(1.13636) / ln(1.005)
N ≈ 25.6 months
Convert to years by dividing by 12 months
25.6 months ÷ 12 months
n ≈ 2.1 years
The logarithm step works because ln(aⁿ) = n × ln(a), which lets us bring the exponent down and solve directly.

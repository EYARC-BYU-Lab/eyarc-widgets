How to read this circuit

Read left to right. Bright mint wires carry 1. Muted wires carry 0. The small numbers show each wire’s value after every stage. Empty slots pass the signal through.

Two inputs, one result. AND and OR span two adjacent wires. Their result goes onto the upper wire. The dot marks a branch: the lower signal feeds the gate and also continues unchanged along the bypass.

Build your own. Drag from the palette to add a gate. Drag an existing gate to move it, or click it and then click its destination. Drop onto another gate to swap them when both fit. Esc cancels a move. Select Eraser to remove a gate.

The Six Characteristics of Professional Skepticism

Questioning mind

Rather than blindly accepting information, ask, “How do we know this is true?”

Suspension of judgment

Avoid rushing to conclusions. Gather and evaluate evidence before forming a judgment.

Search for knowledge

Investigate from every angle. Actively search for more information to understand the entire story.

Interpersonal understanding

Recognize that people may have different motivations, biases, or incentives that influence the information they provide. Consider why someone may be presenting information in a particular way.

Autonomy

Think independently. Form your own opinions and professional judgments rather than simply agreeing with those around you.

Self-esteem

Have confidence in your professional judgment! Be willing to ask difficult questions, challenge explanations, or raise concerns when evidence does not support a conclusion.
